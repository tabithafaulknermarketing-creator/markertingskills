## Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `Planning` | Launch identified but not confirmed; preliminary GTM planning |
| `Confirmed` | Launch date confirmed; full checklist in motion |
| `On Track` | Within 14 days; all pre-launch tasks progressing to schedule |
| `At Risk` | Within 14 days; one or more tasks behind schedule — escalation may be needed |
| `Launched` | Launch complete; monitoring post-launch metrics |
| `Postponed` | Launch date moved; new date TBD |
| `Cancelled` | Launch cancelled |
