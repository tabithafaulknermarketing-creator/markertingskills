## The Problem

You're a founder. Marketing is not your full-time job, but it has to be.

- You write copy at midnight, schedule posts when you remember, and have no system connecting content to pipeline.
- Hiring a CMO costs $200K+. A fractional CMO runs $5–15K/month. Jasper and Copy.ai generate words — not strategy.
- You end up with content that doesn't compound, no clear channel ownership, and a growth loop that depends on your personal hustle.

## What Magister Does

Magister is an autonomous AI CMO built for small SaaS teams. It runs a daily marketing sequence across content, copy, SEO, paid, growth, and sales — drawing on 30+ specialist marketing skills and your product context — without waiting for you to ask.

Every day, Magister produces output: posts written, emails staged, lead magnets briefed, pipeline reviewed, competitors monitored. It knows your ICP, your positioning, and your competitive landscape. It doesn't forget.

---

## Key Capabilities

- **Daily autonomous marketing loop** — Content, SEO, paid, growth, and sales tasks execute on a schedule. No manual triggers required.
- **30+ specialist marketing skills** — Each skill applies a focused framework: CRO, cold email, RevOps, launch strategy, lead magnets, ad creative, email sequences, and more.
- **Product context memory** — Magister retains your ICP, positioning, differentiators, and brand voice across every task. You set it once; every output reflects it.
- **Multi-agent orchestration** — Specialist agents hand off to each other. The Content agent briefs the SEO agent. The RevOps agent routes leads to the right sequence. The system coordinates without you.
- **Open-source skills layer** — The 30+ marketing skills powering Magister are open-source (Marketing Skills on GitHub). Inspect them, extend them, or run them in your own Claude Code environment.
- **Escalation, not noise** — Magister runs autonomously and only asks for human input when a decision requires it: pricing changes, budget approvals, competitor moves that shift strategy.

## Who It's For

Magister is for **technical founders and small SaaS teams** (1–5 people, pre-seed to Series A) who:

- Are building a software product and need marketing to compound, not stall
- Can't justify a full-time CMO or agency retainer yet
- Have tried AI copy tools and found they generate content but not strategy
- Want a system that runs without babysitting — one that knows what to do every morning

Not a fit for: enterprise teams with dedicated marketing departments, agencies looking to resell, or non-SaaS businesses.
