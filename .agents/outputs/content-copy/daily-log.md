### Task 1: Copy QA Checklist (for future runs)

No content was scheduled to go live today. For future runs, apply the following checklist to all copy before marking it ready:

**Pre-publish Copy QA Checklist**

#### Brand Voice
- [ ] Tone is direct, technical, peer-to-peer — no marketing fluff
- [ ] No weasel words: "innovative," "seamless," "robust," "leverage," "optimize"
- [ ] Reads like a builder talking to another builder
- [ ] Active voice throughout
- [ ] Second person ("you") not third ("they" or "our users")

#### Clarity
- [ ] Every sentence is immediately understandable on first read
- [ ] No sentence exceeds 25 words
- [ ] Jargon is either explained or appropriate for the ICP (technical founders)
- [ ] One idea per paragraph

#### Claims & Proof
- [ ] No unsubstantiated superlatives ("best," "leading," "most powerful")
- [ ] Any statistics or claims are sourced and verifiable
- [ ] Social proof is specific (named, attributed, quantified where possible)
- [ ] No fabricated numbers or testimonials

#### SEO (for web/blog content)
- [ ] Primary keyword appears in H1 and at least one H2
- [ ] Meta description is 140-160 characters and includes primary keyword
- [ ] Internal links to relevant skill pages or tool guides
- [ ] Keyword density feels natural — not stuffed

#### CTA
- [ ] One primary CTA per content piece
- [ ] CTA copy follows [Action Verb] + [What They Get] format
- [ ] No "Learn More," "Submit," or "Click Here"
- [ ] Risk reversal present where relevant (free, no credit card, open-source)

#### Copy Sweep (from copy-editing skill — Seven Sweeps)
- [ ] Sweep 1: Clarity — no confusing structures or ambiguity
- [ ] Sweep 2: Voice & Tone — consistent throughout
- [ ] Sweep 3: So What — every claim answers "why should I care?"
- [ ] Sweep 4: Prove It — claims backed by evidence
- [ ] Sweep 5: Specificity — concrete numbers and outcomes, not vague
- [ ] Sweep 6: Heightened Emotion — copy moves the reader
- [ ] Sweep 7: Zero Risk — friction removed near CTA
