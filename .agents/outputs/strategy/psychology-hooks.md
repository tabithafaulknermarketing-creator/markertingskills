## 2026-03-23 — Daily Hook

**Principle: Social Proof + In-Group Identity**

**Behavioural basis:** People are more likely to adopt a behaviour when they see members of their own in-group doing it. For technical founders and indie hackers, the in-group is "builders who ship" — not marketers. The hook is showing that other people *like them* (technical, builder-identity) are already using Marketing Skills to run professional marketing, and it works within their existing workflow.

**Why it's most relevant today:** The top conversion barrier for Marketing Skills is credibility skepticism — "can AI really do proper marketing?" Social proof from people with a technical identity (other founders, hackers) is more persuasive than general testimonials or feature lists. Pairing this with in-group framing ("built for people who code") creates both social proof and identity resonance.

**Application examples:**

- **Copy / Hero headline:** "The marketing toolkit for founders who'd rather ship than pitch. Used by [N] technical founders."
- **CRO (trial CTA):** Add a social proof block near the install CTA: "Joined by founders from [Company], [Company], [Company]"
- **Email subject line:** "How [Founder name] stopped paying £4k/month for an agency and runs marketing with Claude Code"
- **Paid ad angle:** Target: developers/founders on LinkedIn. Hook: "Your coding agent can now do your SEO, CRO, and email marketing. Here's how 200+ founders set it up."

**Tone note:** Avoid "marketers love this." Say "founders who build in public use this." Specificity of in-group matters.
