## Post 11 — Open Banking Advantages
**Subject:** Open banking advantages — Data access / smarter decisions
**Pillar:** Strategic Growth & Brand · Core Product Enablement

---

Open banking isn't the future of lending decisions. It's the present.

Lenders still relying primarily on credit bureau data are making decisions with an incomplete picture. Open banking gives you the full frame: real transaction history, income verification, spending patterns, liability exposure — all with borrower consent, in real time.

What this changes in practice:
→ Faster verification — no waiting for bank statements
→ More accurate income assessment, especially for self-employed borrowers
→ Better risk signals for thin-file or credit-invisible applicants
→ Reduced fraud exposure through source verification

The lenders embracing open banking aren't just making better decisions — they're making them faster, with less friction for the borrower and less manual work for their credit teams.

finPOWER integrates with open banking providers to bring this data directly into the origination and decisioning workflow — so you're not adding a step, you're replacing a slower one.

Smarter data access isn't a regulatory exercise. It's a competitive advantage.
