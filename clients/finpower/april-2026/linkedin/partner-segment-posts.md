## Preferred Partner Post #2 — Secured Signing
**Subject:** Secured Signing portal workflows
**Pillar:** Core Product Enablement · Customer Segment-Centric

---

Every physical signature in your lending workflow is a day you're adding to your settlement timeline.

Digital document execution isn't new — but how deeply it's integrated into your lending workflow makes all the difference between "we have e-signing" and "our settlements are significantly faster because of it."

finPOWER's integration with Secured Signing connects document generation directly to digital execution:
→ Loan documents generated from the system and sent for signing in one step
→ Borrowers sign from any device, without printing or scanning
→ Signed documents returned and stored automatically in the loan file
→ Full audit trail with tamper-evident certificates for every execution
→ Multi-party signing workflows for commercial and asset transactions

The result isn't just faster settlements — it's cleaner files, fewer exceptions, and less operational overhead chasing paper.

Secured Signing is used across financial services because it's built for the compliance requirements of regulated lending. When it's embedded in finPOWER workflows, the operational uplift is immediate.

If you're still running paper-based execution alongside a digital lending platform, this is the integration that closes the gap.

## Preferred Segment Post — High-Volume Lenders
**Subject:** Messaging tools for high-volume lenders
**Pillar:** Customer Segment-Centric · Core Product Enablement

---

High-volume lending has a communication problem — and it's not the volume itself.

When you're processing thousands of loans a month, the communication challenge isn't capacity. It's relevance. Sending the right message, to the right borrower, at exactly the right moment in their loan lifecycle — automatically, at scale, without a team manually managing every send.

This is where purpose-built messaging integration changes the equation for high-volume lenders:

→ **Triggered communications** — messages fire automatically based on loan events (application received, approval, settlement, first payment due, arrears flag)
→ **Channel intelligence** — SMS for urgency, email for detail, push for engagement
→ **Personalisation at scale** — borrower name, loan amount, due date, account details — without individual manual prep
→ **Two-way messaging** — borrowers can respond, and responses route back into the loan management system
→ **Compliance-ready** — full message logs, opt-out management, and regulatory audit trails

For high-volume lenders, the servicing cost per loan has to be low. The lenders achieving this aren't answering more calls — they're preventing them. Proactive, automated communication handles the routine so your team handles the exceptions.

finPOWER's messaging integration capability is built for this reality — because the lenders we work with are doing volume, and volume demands automation done right.
