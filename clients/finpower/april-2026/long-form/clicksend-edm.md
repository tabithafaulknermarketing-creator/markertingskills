Hi [First Name],

There's a moment in every loan lifecycle when a borrower needs to hear from you — and if they don't, they either call your team or they start to disengage.

Proactive communication isn't just a customer experience investment. It's an operational one. Lenders who communicate well at the right moments field fewer inbound calls, see better arrears outcomes, and report higher borrower satisfaction scores.

**Introducing: finPOWER + ClickSend**

finPOWER's integration with ClickSend brings multichannel automated messaging directly into your lending workflows — so the right message goes to the right borrower, at the right moment, without anyone manually triggering it.

**What this means for your operation:**

📱 **SMS for immediacy** — payment reminders, arrears alerts, application updates delivered to mobile where open rates exceed 95%

📧 **Email for detail** — settlement confirmations, document requests, statement notifications with full formatting and attachments

🔔 **Push notifications** — for portal-enabled borrowers, bringing them back to self-service instead of calling your team

🔁 **Two-way messaging** — borrowers can respond to prompts, with replies routing back into your workflow

📋 **Full compliance logging** — every message logged, every opt-out managed, every interaction auditable

**Common workflows our clients are automating with ClickSend:**

- Application acknowledgement and status updates
- Document request follow-up (the manual chase, eliminated)
- Settlement confirmation with next-steps
- Payment due reminders (3 days before, day of, day after)
- Arrears outreach sequences by risk tier
- End-of-term renewal prompts
- Portfolio anniversary check-ins

**The result:** Less time on routine communication. More time on the exceptions that actually need your team.
