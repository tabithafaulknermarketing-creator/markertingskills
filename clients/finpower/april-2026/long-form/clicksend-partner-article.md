Lending operations teams are under constant pressure to do more with less. More borrowers to service. More compliance requirements to satisfy. More communication touchpoints to manage. And less tolerance — from borrowers and regulators alike — for slow, manual, or inconsistent service.

Communication is one of the biggest operational levers most lenders aren't fully pulling. Not because they don't recognise its importance, but because their systems aren't set up to make it efficient.

The finPOWER integration with ClickSend changes this — bringing multichannel, automated borrower communication directly into the lending workflow.

## The Communication Gap in Lending Operations

Consider the communication touchpoints in a typical loan lifecycle:

- Application received acknowledgement
- Conditional approval notification
- Document request and follow-up
- Settlement confirmation
- First payment reminder
- Ongoing statement notifications
- Arrears outreach (initial, escalation, resolution)
- End-of-term options communication
- Renewal or refinance prompts

In many lending operations, a significant portion of these touchpoints are either manually triggered, handled ad hoc, or not happening at all. Teams are busy managing exceptions and the communication workflow falls through the cracks.

The result: borrowers who feel uninformed, servicing teams fielding preventable inbound calls, and a borrower experience that doesn't reflect the quality of the lender's underlying product.

## What Integration Actually Means

There's a meaningful difference between having a messaging tool and having messaging integrated into your lending platform.

A standalone messaging tool requires someone to manage it — building audiences, scheduling sends, tracking responses. It adds capability but also adds workload.

When ClickSend is integrated into finPOWER's lending workflows, the dynamic changes:

**Triggered automation.** Messages fire automatically based on events in the loan lifecycle. Approval triggers a congratulations and next-steps message. A missed payment triggers a tiered outreach sequence. End-of-term approaches and a renewal conversation starts — without anyone manually initiating it.

**Personalisation without manual prep.** Every message can draw on live data from the loan management system: borrower name, loan amount, payment date, outstanding balance, account manager details. The personalisation isn't a marketing exercise — it's accurate, relevant information delivered automatically.

**Channel selection by message type.** SMS for urgent, time-sensitive communications where open rates are high. Email for detailed correspondence with attachments. Push notifications for borrowers using self-service portals. The channel matches the message, not the other way around.

**Two-way capability.** ClickSend supports inbound messaging, meaning borrowers can respond to SMS prompts and those responses route back into the workflow — triggering follow-up actions or routing to the right team member.

**Compliance-ready records.** Every message sent, delivered, opened, and responded to is logged. Opt-out management is handled automatically. For regulated lenders, this creates the communication audit trail that compliance frameworks require without additional manual documentation.

## Impact on Lender Operations

The operational benefits of integrated messaging show up in several measurable ways.

**Reduced inbound servicing volume.** When borrowers are proactively informed — about their application status, payment due dates, and account events — they make fewer inbound enquiries. Lenders typically see a measurable reduction in call centre volume within the first 90 days of deploying automated communication workflows.

**Improved arrears outcomes.** Early, consistent arrears communication is one of the most significant factors in recovery rates. When arrears outreach sequences start automatically at the first missed payment — rather than when a collections team gets to the account — the engagement window is broader and outcomes improve.

**Faster document collection.** Document request and follow-up sequences are one of the most labour-intensive parts of origination for many lenders. Automated SMS and email follow-up for outstanding documents, triggered from the LOS, reduces the manual chase and shortens settlement timelines.

**Better borrower experience at scale.** Borrowers who receive consistent, personalised, timely communication are more satisfied — and more likely to return. For lenders competing on experience as much as rate, this is a genuine differentiator.

## High-Volume Lending: Where This Matters Most

The compounding value of integrated messaging is most visible at high volume. A lender processing 500 loans a month and a lender processing 5,000 loans a month have very different communication challenges — but the same underlying need: every borrower needs to feel serviced, and it's not possible to do that manually at scale.

ClickSend's infrastructure is built for volume. Millions of messages, delivered reliably, across SMS, email, and direct mail. When that capability is connected to finPOWER's workflow engine, high-volume lenders get the communication consistency their borrowers deserve without the headcount that would otherwise require.
