## Partner Spotlight: ClickSend Communications

### Connecting Lenders with Borrowers at Every Moment That Matters

---

**The Partner**

ClickSend is a global communications platform trusted by thousands of businesses across financial services, logistics, healthcare, and retail. With infrastructure built for high-volume, time-sensitive message delivery, ClickSend enables organisations to communicate with their customers via SMS, email, voice, fax, and direct mail — from a single API.

In the lending industry, ClickSend is used by financial institutions across Australia, New Zealand, the United Kingdom, and North America to power borrower communications across the entire loan lifecycle.

---

**Why finPOWER Partners with ClickSend**

At finPOWER, we build technology for lenders who take their operations seriously. That means the integrations we recommend to our clients need to meet the same standard: reliable infrastructure, compliance-ready architecture, and genuine operational value — not just connectivity for its own sake.

ClickSend meets that standard.

The combination of multichannel capability (SMS, email, and push notifications from a single integration point), high-volume delivery reliability, and two-way messaging functionality makes ClickSend the messaging partner of choice for lenders who need their communication workflows to perform at scale.

We've integrated ClickSend directly into finPOWER's lending workflow engine — so lenders aren't connecting two separate systems. Communication triggers live inside finPOWER, using live loan data, and messages are delivered via ClickSend's infrastructure without any manual intervention.

---

**What the Integration Delivers**

**Lifecycle-triggered messaging**
Every event in the loan lifecycle — application received, approval issued, document requested, payment due, arrears flag raised, end-of-term approaching — can trigger an automated, personalised message to the borrower. No manual send. No communication falling through the cracks.

**Multichannel reach**
Different messages suit different channels. Payment reminders with 95%+ SMS open rates. Settlement confirmations with full document detail via email. Portal action prompts via push notification. finPOWER + ClickSend gives lenders the ability to route each message type to the channel where it gets the best response.

**Personalisation at scale**
Every message draws on live finPOWER data — borrower name, loan reference, payment amount, due date, account manager details. The personalisation isn't cosmetic; it's accurate, relevant information that makes the communication feel like it was written for that borrower specifically. Automatically. At any volume.

**Two-way communication**
Borrowers can respond to SMS prompts, and those responses are captured and routed within the workflow. This enables automated self-service triage — a borrower texting back "HELP" gets a different response path than one texting "PAID" — without a team member managing the conversation in real time.

**Compliance-ready architecture**
Every message is logged with delivery status, open rate, and response data. Opt-out management is handled automatically and in compliance with Australian Spam Act and telecommunications regulations. For regulated lenders, the audit trail is built in.

**Getting Started**

Activating ClickSend within finPOWER is straightforward. There is no custom development required. The integration uses ClickSend's API credentials, configured within finPOWER's integration settings, and communication triggers are set up using finPOWER's workflow engine.

Most lenders have their first automated workflows live within a day of connecting their ClickSend account.

For lenders new to ClickSend, finPOWER can facilitate introductions to the ClickSend team directly. For existing ClickSend customers, the finPOWER integration is additive — you keep your existing account and messaging history, with finPOWER now driving your lending-specific automation.
