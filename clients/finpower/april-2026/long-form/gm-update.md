**Q2 2026 Pipeline Update**
*From the General Manager*
*April 2026*

---

As we move into Q2, I want to give the team a clear picture of where we stand on pipeline, what's driving the conversations we're having, and where the focus needs to be over the next 90 days.

**Pipeline Position**

Our Q2 pipeline is tracking ahead of the same period last year, with a notable increase in qualified opportunities across the asset finance and commercial lending segments. The increased activity reflects the investment we've made in market presence through Q1 — the content program, the webinar series, and the direct outreach effort from the sales team are creating more first conversations than we had 12 months ago.

The challenge remains conversion velocity. We're generating more top-of-funnel activity but the average time from first meeting to commercial agreement has extended slightly. The primary driver appears to be increased due diligence from prospects — more stakeholders involved in platform decisions, more detailed security and compliance requirements, and longer internal evaluation periods. This is the market, not a weakness in our positioning. The response is to ensure our evaluation support is as strong as our first impression.

**Key Themes in Q2 Pipeline Conversations**

Three themes are appearing consistently across pipeline conversations this quarter:

*Integration capability.* Prospects are asking harder questions about our API architecture and pre-built integrations earlier in the process. This is a positive signal — it means buyers are thinking strategically, not just feature-comparing. Our API-first positioning is resonating. We need to ensure our commercial team can speak to it fluently and has the technical support to back it up in evaluation.

*Segment fit.* We're seeing more multi-segment prospects — lenders operating across consumer, asset, and commercial who need a platform that can accommodate the full portfolio. Our configurable workflow capability is a genuine differentiator here. The risk is that we don't demonstrate it clearly enough during evaluation; prospects default to assuming we're better for one segment than another.

*Roadmap confidence.* Buyers want to know where the product is going. The innovation story we're telling in Q2 — BNPL enhancements, portal improvements, open banking integration — is landing well. Continuing to communicate the roadmap externally, not just in sales conversations, will support pipeline development in Q3.

**Q2 Focus Areas**

For the remainder of Q2, the priorities are:

1. **Accelerate open opportunities** — the team has eight qualified opportunities at proposal stage. These are the priority. Full support available.
2. **Deepen partner referral pipeline** — our ClickSend and Secured Signing relationships are generating warm referrals. We should formalise the referral process and set a target for Q3 partner-sourced introductions.
3. **Convert webinar attendance to conversations** — April's innovation spotlight webinar has strong registration numbers. The follow-up protocol needs to be tight. Every attendee should hear from us within 48 hours.

We're in a strong position. The product is competitive, the market is active, and the team is performing. The work in Q2 is about converting the pipeline we've built.

More detail available in the CRM dashboard. Questions to me directly.
