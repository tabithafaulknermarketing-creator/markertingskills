### Why Portals Succeed (and Why Most Don't)

The most common failure mode for borrower and investor portals isn't missing features. It's a bad first-login experience.

When a borrower creates an account, navigates to their loan details, and finds something confusing or incomplete, they close the tab and call your team instead. That pattern repeats. The portal never earns the habit.

The portals with high adoption rates have three things in common:

**1. Fast, frictionless onboarding.** First login takes under 60 seconds. No IT ticket, no call to activate, no seven-step verification process that requires a desktop browser.

**2. Immediate value.** The first screen shows the borrower exactly what they came for — their balance, their next payment, their documents. Nothing buried.

**3. A reason to come back.** Notifications that bring borrowers back to the portal when something has changed, rather than leaving them to wonder if they should check.

For investor portals, the driver is slightly different. Investors return when the data is current and complete. If the portal is still delivering the same quarterly PDF report that used to come via email, you haven't created an experience — you've changed the delivery mechanism.

### Integration: The Other Side of the Adoption Story

Portal adoption and integration capability are more connected than they might seem. When your lending platform integrates well with the tools borrowers and investors are already using — messaging platforms, document signing tools, identity verification services — the portal becomes the natural place for every interaction.

The lenders with the highest portal adoption are, almost without exception, the ones with the most connected underlying platform.

At finPOWER, our Q1 integration work has focused on:

- **ClickSend:** Triggered SMS and email messaging from loan lifecycle events, bringing borrowers back to the portal to take action rather than calling your team
- **Secured Signing:** Document execution embedded in the portal workflow — borrowers sign from the same interface they use to check their loan details
- **Open banking providers:** Verification workflows that happen inside the portal experience, not as a separate browser session

Each of these integrations reduces a reason to go somewhere else — which is what portal adoption ultimately requires.
